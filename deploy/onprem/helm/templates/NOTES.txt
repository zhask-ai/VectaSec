READ THIS BEFORE YOU CALL THIS INSTALLED
────────────────────────────────────────

1. MIGRATIONS ARE NOT APPLIED BY THIS CHART.
   Nothing here runs supabase/migrations/*.sql. The engine will start, /health will report
   {"db": false} or the pods will error on first query, until you apply them yourself:

     psql "$DATABASE_URL" -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 \
       -f supabase/migrations/20260717000001_core_schema.sql \
       -f supabase/migrations/20260717000002_platform_schema.sql

   Your Postgres must have pgcrypto, pgmq and pg_cron, and the anon/authenticated/service_role
   roles. Stock Postgres has none of that — migration 2 fails on line 16.

2. THE ENGINE API AUTHENTICATES NOBODY.
   It takes the tenant from an X-VectaSec-Tenant header and believes it, while holding a
   BYPASSRLS connection. Anything that reaches {{ .Values.ingress.paths.engine }} can read and
   modify every tenant.

3. THE CONSOLE IMAGE IS HOST-SPECIFIC.
   {{ .Values.web.image.repository }}:{{ .Values.web.image.tag }} has NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL
   and NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY compiled into its browser bundle. supabase.url and
   supabase.anonKey in values.yaml do NOT repoint it. If that image was not built for
   {{ .Values.publicUrl }}, the console will load and then fail every query. Build your own:
   see deploy/onprem/Dockerfile.web.
